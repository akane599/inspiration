# Entry Points
*Last Updated: 2026-10-20*

## Production entry points

Screens: `AppShell` ([`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/shell/AppShell.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/shell/AppShell.kt)) hosts the five routes; each route is `@Composable fun XxxRoute(deps: XxxDependencies, modifier)` in its feature module ([`CockpitRoute`](../../feature/dashboard/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/dashboard/CockpitRoute.kt), [`XRayRoute`](../../feature/xray/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/xray/XRayRoute.kt), [`SleepLabRoute`](../../feature/sleeplab/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/sleeplab/SleepLabRoute.kt), [`HealthRoute`](../../feature/health/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/health/HealthRoute.kt), [`ToolkitRoute`](../../feature/toolkit/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/toolkit/ToolkitRoute.kt)).

- **`MainActivity`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/MainActivity.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/MainActivity.kt). The exported launcher activity enables edge-to-edge with a forced-dark `SystemBarStyle.dark(TRANSPARENT)` for both bars (the theme is always dark AMOLED), wraps `AppShell` in `InspirationTheme` from `:core:designsystem`, calls `window.setHideOverlayWindows(true)` (manifest permission `HIDE_OVERLAY_WINDOWS`, audit R-10), so other apps' non-system overlays are hidden over the privileged confirmations (the touch guards only see an overlay under the touch point), and calls `InspirationApplication.captureAppOpen()` from `onStart`. The Shizuku permission re-read (`shizukuManager.refresh()`; a grant made in the Shizuku app sends no callback) is no longer on Main there: it runs first inside the app-open capture coroutine, before `AppContainer.refresh()` (R-08). Its private `DestinationRoute` maps each `Destination` to `CockpitRoute`, `XRayRoute`, `SleepLabRoute`, `HealthRoute` or `ToolkitRoute`, handing each one the container as only its own dependencies interface. There are no deep links, so nothing selects a destination or package from Intent extras.
- **`InspirationApplication`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/InspirationApplication.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/InspirationApplication.kt). Registered in [`app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`](../../app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml). It first exempts only `Sensor.getHandle()` (`AndroidSensorCatalog.GET_HANDLE_SIGNATURE`) from the hidden-API policy through `HiddenApiBypass`, never the whole process (N-26). It then creates `AppContainer`, starts the Shizuku state watcher and `AppContainer.start()` (reconcile of PENDING toolkit rows on every Shizuku Ready), registers the non-exported runtime receiver, and `captureAppOpen()` (arms the charging job, then launches in the application scope) re-reads Shizuku state and delegates to the container's debounced `refresh()`.
- **`AppContainer`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/AppContainer.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/AppContainer.kt). Manual DI root. Creates `ShizukuManagerImpl`, `InspirationDatabase`, `RoomSnapshotStore`, `AndroidTelemetrySource`, `SnapshotCapturer`, `LiveTelemetry`, `RefreshCoordinator`, `AnalysisSession` and `ToolkitActions` in the application scope, and implements `CockpitDependencies`, `XRayDependencies`, `SleepLabDependencies`, `HealthDependencies` and `ToolkitDependencies`. The diagnostic dictionary is a `Preloaded` IO load (never parsed on the caller's thread); `ruleEngine` is lazy. See `modules.md` for the shell/di breakdown.
- **`RuntimeCaptureReceiver`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/RuntimeCaptureReceiver.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/RuntimeCaptureReceiver.kt). Dynamically registered with `RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED`; handles screen, power, and battery broadcasts according to `CapturePolicy`, and emits a status tick after the capture through `statusTicker.emitAfter` (also on a miss or timeout). Work runs through `BroadcastCaptureRunner`. The guard is sized from the costliest plan the action can get (`forBroadcast(..., screenOffForMs = Long.MAX_VALUE)` then `CapturePolicy.broadcastGuardMs`: the read deadline plus a 1.5 s persist budget; 6 s + 1.5 s for a bind-on-demand screen capture). A SCREEN_ON that is cold with Shizuku ready settles its real plan inside the capture coroutine, after `AppContainer.screenOffForMs()` reads how long the screen was off from the stored screen captures (R-03). `SnapshotCapturer.captureFor(plan)` takes a light snapshot for the same trigger when a full capture misses.
- **`BootCaptureReceiver`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/BootCaptureReceiver.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/BootCaptureReceiver.kt), manifest-declared for `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`. It first calls `StatusNotificationController.sync` synchronously (inside the boot/replace FGS exemption, before `goAsync`). It then writes a light snapshot (wrapped in `statusTicker.emitAfter`, so the status notification redraws, R-14), BOOT for `BOOT_COMPLETED` and `PACKAGE_REPLACED` for `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED` (pure `BootCapturePlan`, N-14), and arms the persisted charging job. Android 15 also sends `BOOT_COMPLETED` when a force-stopped app leaves the stopped state, so a BOOT row does not prove a reboot. Consumers detect boots from `bootCount`/`elapsedRealtime`.
- **`StatusNotificationService`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/status/StatusNotificationService.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/status/StatusNotificationService.kt), manifest-declared `exported=false`, `foregroundServiceType="specialUse"` (with `PROPERTY_SPECIAL_USE_FGS_SUBTYPE`). The resident status notification (channel `status`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_IMMEDIATE`): posts a placeholder via `ServiceCompat.startForeground`, re-checks `shouldRun(enabled, blocked)` (sticky restarts bypass the controller), then collects `AppContainer.statusTicks.redrawsFromNow()` (`StatusTicks.kt`: subscribes with `onSubscription` before the first redraw, so a capture finishing during it is not lost, R-14), redrawing from stored snapshots (`unplugWindowSnapshots` over 30 d: only the rows from the unplug mark's last screen/boot capture, bounded in SQL) plus one fresh `light()` read through `UnplugSummaryCalculator`. No battery receiver, timer or Shizuku binding. A `StatusStartGate` (`StatusStartDecision.kt`, Main thread only) marks a `startForegroundService` call not yet handled by `onStartCommand`; while it is pending, `sync` does not send a stop (stopping before `startForeground` crashes the app), and the pending start re-reads `shouldRun` and stops itself (R-06). Started/stopped by [`StatusNotificationController.sync`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/status/StatusNotificationController.kt) from `MainActivity.onStart`, after the one-time POST_NOTIFICATIONS prompt, the Toolkit switch (`AppContainer.statusSync`) and `BootCaptureReceiver`.
- **`ChargingCaptureJobService`** — [`app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/ChargingCaptureJobService.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/ChargingCaptureJobService.kt), manifest-declared with `BIND_JOB_SERVICE`. It runs bounded full capture/history/prune work, handles cancellation, and re-arms through [`ChargingCaptureScheduler.kt`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/ChargingCaptureScheduler.kt). The body is [`runChargeJob`](../../app/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/capture/ChargeJobRun.kt) (re-arms exactly once from `finally`, skipped when an immediate boot/replace arm happened during the run; the scheduler repeats the generation check and submission under one lock) around `runChargeJobCapture` in `ChargingCaptureRunner.kt`, which does one full read per run (`SnapshotCapturer.ingestHistory` → `HistoryIngest`, reused by `captureFull(ingest)`) and calls `onHistoryStored` (`StoreTicks.tick`) once history is stored, so visible screens reload (N-04).
- **`TelemetryUserService`** — [`core/shizuku-ipc/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/ipc/service/TelemetryUserService.kt`](../../core/shizuku-ipc/src/main/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/ipc/service/TelemetryUserService.kt). Shizuku constructs it as a shell-UID user service; every AIDL method enforces the caller UID before reading privileged data; the typed toolkit txns 20–30 additionally go through `ToolkitExecutor` (validation, hard-deny rules, fixed argv).

## Debug entry points

- **`ProbeReceiver`** — [`app/src/debug/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/debug/ProbeReceiver.kt`](../../app/src/debug/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/debug/ProbeReceiver.kt), registered by [`app/src/debug/AndroidManifest.xml`](../../app/src/debug/AndroidManifest.xml). It accepts `PROBE_REQUEST_PERMISSION`, `PROBE`, `PROBE_CAPTURE`, `PROBE_DIFF`, and `PROBE_INSIGHTS` (the last two take the string extra `reference_kind`), performs bounded IPC/calibration/capture/diff/insight/database checks with a 10 s deadline, and emits structured `InspirationProbe` logcat output. `PROBE_INSIGHTS` logs interval metrics, insights, notEvaluable reasons, the rendered en Battery Story and top-wakelock dictionary lookups.
  - **In-process reads:** `PROBE` reads power monitors and deviceidle in-process (`PowerStatsClient`, `DeviceIdleReader`; AIDL txns 4/5 are gone). Before that it adds its own debug-only hidden-API exemptions (`IN_PROCESS_HIDDEN_API_PREFIXES`: the batterystats/usage-stats classes plus `IPowerStatsService`, `IDeviceIdleController` and `IPowerManager`).
  - **Store tick:** a saved `PROBE_CAPTURE` ticks `StoreTicks`.
  - **Access:** the receiver is debug-only and protected by `android.permission.DUMP`.
- **`DesignGalleryActivity`** — [`app/src/debug/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/debug/DesignGalleryActivity.kt`](../../app/src/debug/kotlin/com/akane/inspiration/debug/DesignGalleryActivity.kt), declared `exported` in the debug manifest (no intent filter, no extras). It renders `SynchronizedScrubbingChart` and `SleepHypnogram` with synthetic data from `debug/gallery/GalleryData.kt` (`GalleryDataGenerator`) for on-device interaction and frame-stat checks; `debug/gallery/RecomposeCounter.kt` (`RecompositionCounter`, inline `RecomposeCounter`) tallies recompositions and logs them under logcat tag `InspirationGallery`. Launch with `adb shell am start -n com.akane.inspiration/.debug.DesignGalleryActivity`.

## Internal capture handoff

`InspirationApplication.onCreate()` creates the container and registers `RuntimeCaptureReceiver`. `CapturePolicy.forBroadcast` turns each broadcast into a plan: FULL (without a history ingest) when the service is warm; FULL with an on-demand bind (`BROADCAST_BIND_FULL_DEADLINE_MS` = 6 s) for SCREEN_OFF and for SCREEN_ON after a screen-off of at least `MIN_SCREEN_OFF_MS` (30 min) while Shizuku is Ready; LIGHT otherwise. `SnapshotCapturer` calls `AndroidTelemetrySource`, merges source availability, and writes through `RoomSnapshotStore`; a missed full capture is recorded in `capture_miss`. The charging service also uses the history cursor and last plug/job rows to make its persisted run idempotent per plug session.

The Shizuku manager publishes its service as a `StateFlow<ITelemetryUserService?>`; capture uses `withTelemetryService()` for bounded on-demand binding, while a UI may use `holdBinding()` to keep it warm.
